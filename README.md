# E-commerce Customer Insights

This project focuses on analyzing customer behavior based on e-commerce platform serving both B2B and B2C customers. The project is based on two years of companies raw customer data (22.2.2023-21.2.2025) and the goal was to transform the raw data into actionable business insights and actions.

**The Business Problem**

The e-commerce platform lacked a structured view of their customer base.
* Which of the customers are the most critical and how we ensure that they stay?
* How can we identify and react to customer churn?
## 📊 Insights & Actions

* **Customer Segmentation:** The analysis identified 3 main customer segments. (Active customers with high frequency, Active customers with low frequency and passive customers).

**Key Insights:**
* We need to keep our **active customers with high frequency** (A-class customers). These are the most important customers of our platform.
* Our platform is losing more customers then it is getting new ones (40% of customers are passive and only 38% are new)

**Actions:**
* We should send **VIP-benefits** for A-class customers and make a **loyalty program** for B & C so these clients would stay with us and not change to a competitor.
* For passive customers we should start a **Win-back program** which sends personalized email containing special offer for next order and purchase recommendations based on the clients buying history.
* After first purchase in our platform we should send email containing purchase recommendations based on the first order and advertisements about our loyalty program.

By implementing these actions, we can recapture passive revenue and increase customer lifetime value.
